# CROWNFALL — PROLOGUE STORY DESIGN

**Title:** The Crown's Call  
**Leads into:** Chapter I — Forgotten Shore

> This document defines the intended story experience. It does not authorize the coding agent to invent unresolved lore. Any detail marked OPEN must remain undecided until the project owner approves it.

## 1. Story premise

Aetherion was once protected by **The First Light**. After an event known as **The Great Eclipse**, the First Light vanished; kingdoms collapsed, civilizations were lost, and hostile creatures spread through the world.

Since childhood, Monarch has occasionally felt an unfamiliar energy within himself. Once, it manifested briefly and uncontrollably. He never understood it and could not reproduce it.

As an adult, he begins searching for an explanation. During his travels, he repeatedly encounters the same royal symbol on ancient remains. Following these clues leads him to a forgotten royal ruin, where he discovers an ancient crown resting on an altar.

Touching the crown awakens the dormant inherited power within him and triggers a fragmented vision of an ancient kingdom. The vision raises more questions than it answers.

Monarch continues his search by sea, but a storm wrecks his ship. He awakens on an unfamiliar island: Forgotten Shore. The crown remains with him; the story does not explain how it survived the shipwreck.

The Prologue ends when the player regains control on Forgotten Shore. Chapter I begins with survival, exploration, and the gradual establishment of a settlement.

## 2. Agreed story direction

- Monarch is human and has dormant inherited power.
- Since childhood, he has felt an unusual instinct/energy and experienced one small, uncontrolled manifestation. He does not understand or control it.
- As an adult, he searches for an explanation of those experiences.
- The same unresolved royal symbol appears repeatedly on ancient remains and draws him toward the ruins.
- The crown is found on an altar in a hidden chamber.
- The crown is a **trigger/key**, not the source of Monarch's power.
- Touching the crown awakens the inherited power and causes a fragmented vision.
- The vision may show an ancient kingdom, a ruler's silhouette, the recurring symbol, fading light, and interrupted images of destruction.
- Monarch travels by sea, suffers a shipwreck, and lands on Forgotten Shore.
- The crown remains with Monarch after the shipwreck; do not explain its survival in dialogue.
- Storytelling style is **balanced**: cinematic moments, short dialogue, gameplay, environmental storytelling, and audio.
- Bloodline/royal identity and the origin of the power are revealed gradually, not explained in the Prologue.
- Having inherited power does not automatically make Monarch a worthy ruler.

## 3. Narrative pillars

1. **Personal mystery first.** Monarch wants to understand what he has experienced; he is not initially seeking a throne.
2. **Clues, not answers.** Symbols, ruins, the crown, and the vision hint at a connection without solving it.
3. **The crown awakens, it does not grant.** The power already exists within Monarch.
4. **Keep Monarch grounded.** He is curious and observant, not a trained mage or an experienced king.
5. **Let gameplay carry the story.** Avoid long exposition and unnecessary cutscenes.
6. **Leadership must be earned.** Bloodline and power are not proof of good leadership.

## 4. Prologue sequence

| # | Beat | Intended experience |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | The Lost World | A short opening shows Aetherion's former glory, The First Light, and the Great Eclipse. Its cause remains unknown. |
| 02 | A Faint Resonance | A short childhood scene shows one small uncontrolled manifestation of Monarch's power. |
| 03 | The Wanderer | Adult Monarch travels and searches for an explanation of his strange experiences. |
| 04 | The Repeated Mark | The same royal symbol appears on several ancient remains. Monarch recognizes the pattern. |
| 05 | The Forgotten Ruins | The clues lead to a forgotten royal ruin and a hidden chamber. |
| 06 | The Crown's Call | Monarch finds the crown on an altar. Touching it awakens his dormant power and triggers a fragmented vision. |
| 07 | The Unfinished Journey | Monarch leaves the ruins and continues his search by sea. |
| 08 | The Shipwreck | A storm wrecks the ship. Whether the storm is connected to the crown remains unknown. |
| 09 | Forgotten Shore | Monarch wakes on the island. The crown is still with him. The player regains control and Chapter I begins. |

### Beat 01 — The Lost World

**Visual direction**
- Show Aetherion during its prosperous era.
- Suggest the presence/protection of The First Light.
- Transition through the Great Eclipse into abandoned kingdoms and ruined landscapes.

**Candidate narration**
> Long ago, Aetherion flourished beneath the light of an ancient power.  
> Kingdoms rose. Civilizations prospered. And the world endured beneath the First Light.  
> Then came the Great Eclipse.  
> The light vanished. Kingdoms fell. And the world was left to the ages.

Do not explain who or what caused the Great Eclipse.

### Beat 02 — A Faint Resonance

**Proposed staging — not a fixed lore fact**
- Show Monarch as a child near an old stone wall or ancient stone remains.
- He pauses after feeling a faint warmth or low resonance.
- When he touches the stone, a few cracks emit a dim golden light for a brief moment.
- The light fades. He tries to understand or repeat the phenomenon but cannot.
- Transition to Monarch as an adult.

No large magical display, spellcasting, combat ability, or conscious control. The exact physical location and reason the stone reacts remain OPEN.

### Beat 03 — The Wanderer

- Give the player control early for movement and observation.
- Show that Monarch has travelled before through worn equipment, notes, or collected fragments.
- His search is motivated by the unexplained childhood experiences.
- Keep dialogue sparse.

**Candidate internal line**
> Some things are difficult to forget.  
> Especially the things you cannot explain.

### Beat 04 — The Repeated Mark

- Present the same recognizable visual symbol on different objects or structures.
- Vary the condition: carved on a pillar, partly covered by moss, or visible on a broken artifact.
- Let the player inspect the objects.
- Monarch notices that the appearances are connected.

**Candidate internal line**
> I've seen this mark before...  
> Why does it keep appearing?

The symbol's visual design must be consistent, but its name, dynasty, meaning, and historical context remain OPEN.

### Beat 05 — The Forgotten Ruins

- The repeated mark leads Monarch to a larger ancient ruin.
- The player explores the environment and follows visual clues.
- The route leads into a hidden chamber.
- A crown rests on an altar at the center.

Do not add an NPC who explains the kingdom's history. Environmental storytelling should do most of the work.

### Beat 06 — The Crown's Call

- Allow the player to approach and interact with the crown.
- On contact, the dormant inherited power awakens.
- Use a short sound and visual motif that subtly recalls the childhood resonance.
- Show a fragmented vision: an ancient kingdom, a ruler's silhouette, the recurring symbol, fading light, and interrupted destruction.
- End the vision abruptly.

**Candidate dialogue after the vision**
> What... was that?  
> I've never seen those places before.

Do not reveal the ruler's identity, the kingdom/dynasty name, the source of the inherited power, the crown's origin, or the cause of the Great Eclipse.

### Beat 07 — The Unfinished Journey

- Monarch leaves the ruin with more questions than answers.
- He continues searching for further traces and travels by sea.
- The exact destination, ship's origin, and reason for choosing that route remain OPEN.

### Beat 08 — The Shipwreck

- A storm overtakes the ship.
- The vessel loses control and is wrecked.
- Use a concise cinematic transition; do not build a controllable ship or full weather simulation for this scene.
- Fade out before the moment becomes excessively long or graphic.

The storm's connection, if any, to the crown is deliberately unresolved.

### Beat 09 — Forgotten Shore

- Monarch wakes on the beach.
- Show the wreckage and an unfamiliar island.
- Keep the crown with him and show it briefly as a visual continuity detail.
- Do not add dialogue explaining how it survived.
- Return control promptly so the player can inspect the beach and begin survival gameplay.

**Title card**
> CROWNFALL  
> CHAPTER I — FORGOTTEN SHORE

The optional tagline “A new kingdom begins” should be reviewed against the Chapter I opening; Monarch initially seeks survival, not immediate kingship.

## 5. Character and dialogue rules

### Monarch's progression
- **Childhood:** senses something unusual; cannot understand or control it.
- **Adult search:** curious, observant, and persistent; wants an explanation.
- **After the vision:** surprised and confused, not instantly convinced he is royal.
- **After shipwreck:** focuses on immediate survival.

### Dialogue rules
- **Default dialogue language: English.** All authored in-game dialogue for the Prologue and later chapters uses English as the source/canonical text by default.
- Keep lines short and natural.
- Do not repeat information that the player can plainly see.
- Avoid having Monarch declare himself a king, chosen one, or rightful heir.
- Avoid exposition that explains the entire world or the vision.
- Use pauses, expressions, audio, and environment to carry emotion.

## 6. Gameplay and pacing

| Beat | Player control | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| The Lost World | No | Short opening cinematic |
| A Faint Resonance | No | Short flashback |
| The Wanderer | Yes | Movement and observation |
| The Repeated Mark | Yes | Exploration and object inspection |
| The Forgotten Ruins | Yes | Exploration and interaction |
| The Crown's Call | Interaction then cinematic | Altar activation and vision |
| The Unfinished Journey | Limited or cinematic | Transition toward the voyage |
| The Shipwreck | No | Short storm/shipwreck sequence |
| Forgotten Shore | Yes | Return control and begin survival gameplay |

Keep the Prologue concise. The player should not spend a long period watching cinematics. The vision is the emotional peak; the landing is the handoff to the existing game.

## 7. Art and audio needs

**Visual assets**
- Aetherion at its former height and after the Great Eclipse.
- Child and adult Monarch with consistent visual identity.
- Ancient stone surface for the childhood manifestation.
- One consistent royal-symbol design.
- Ancient props and repeated-symbol locations.
- Ruin exterior, hidden chamber, altar, and crown.
- Ancient kingdom/ruler/ruin fragments for the vision.
- Ship, ocean, storm, shipwreck debris, and Forgotten Shore beach.

**Audio**
- Opening music that changes from prosperity to loss.
- Quiet ruin and exploration ambience.
- A subtle resonance motif for the childhood event.
- A related but stronger motif at the crown.
- Distorted/fragmented audio for the vision.
- Wind, waves, storm, and calm beach ambience after the wreck.

Reuse assets and effects where practical. Placeholder assets must be labeled and must not imply unapproved lore.

## 8. Prologue-to-Chapter-I boundary

**Prologue ends when:**
- Monarch wakes on Forgotten Shore.
- The player understands that he is stranded.
- The crown remains with him without verbal explanation.
- Control returns to the player.

**Chapter I begins when:**
- The player inspects the surroundings.
- Survival needs become the immediate priority.
- Exploration and settlement-building emerge as practical responses to the island's conditions.

Monarch should not immediately announce an intention to establish a kingdom. The settlement should grow from survival into leadership through gameplay.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

1. The player understands that Aetherion once flourished and was changed by the Great Eclipse.
2. The childhood scene shows only one small, uncontrolled manifestation.
3. The adult Monarch's search is motivated by his unexplained experiences.
4. The same unresolved symbol appears on multiple ancient remains.
5. Monarch discovers the crown on an altar in a hidden chamber.
6. Touching the crown awakens inherited power and triggers a fragmented vision.
7. The vision does not reveal protected mysteries.
8. Monarch continues his search by sea and suffers a shipwreck.
9. He wakes on Forgotten Shore with the crown still with him; no dialogue explains its survival.
10. Player control returns promptly on Forgotten Shore.
11. No dialogue, data, or visual asset silently converts an OPEN detail into canon.
12. The Prologue does not damage the existing Chapter I gameplay or save flow.
